Solitaire Help – How to Play

🎯 Objective

Move all 52 cards to the four Foundation piles (♥ ♦ ♣ ♠), built from Ace up to King by suit. Fill all four foundations to win!

🃏 The Piles

PileDescription
StockFace-down draw pile. Click to draw a card.
WasteShows the last card drawn. Can be played onto tableau or foundations.
Foundations ×4Build A → K by suit. Win when all four are complete.
Tableau ×7Seven columns where most gameplay happens.

📜 Rules

  1. Tableau columns are built in descending order with alternating colors (e.g. red 6 on black 7).
  2. Foundations are built in ascending order (A, 2 … K) by the same suit.
  3. Only a King (or a sequence starting with a King) may be placed on an empty tableau column.
  4. Click the Stock to draw one card to the Waste pile.
  5. When the Stock is empty, click it to recycle the Waste pile back to the Stock.

🏆 Scoring

ActionPoints
